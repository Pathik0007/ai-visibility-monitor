How it works

1

The questions your customers actually ask

Not just "best dentist in North Ryde" -- real intents for your kind of business: "emergency dentist open on weekends", "plumber available today", "how much is a check-up", "good with nervous patients". Each question is tagged with what it tests (urgency, price, a specific service, trust), and we never mention your business by name -- the score shows whether assistants recommend you unprompted.

2

Asked to five assistants, as a local customer

ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini search the live web as someone in your suburb would (Gemini also uses Google Maps). DeepSeek answers from what it learned in training. We keep the pages they cite.

3

Scored, not guessed

We check whether you're actually recommended (spelling variants like "Ace's"/"Aces" count; "I couldn't find information about X" doesn't), where you rank, who's named instead, and what the assistants say about each business.

4

A plan for the three things assistants rely on

Every step is tagged to one of them and tied to what happened in your check -- a question you were missed on, a site the assistants read that doesn't list you, a competitor with more reviews.

What counts: it's not only Google

Each assistant gets local business information from different places, so a Google Business Profile alone isn't enough:

  • Gemini answers local questions from Google Maps -- your Google Business Profile, reviews and photos.
  • ChatGPT doesn't read Google Business Profiles directly. Its local answers lean on Bing (Bing Places), business websites, articles and directories.
  • Claude and Perplexity search the live web: your website, directories, review sites and local articles.
  • Siri and Apple Maps use Apple Business Connect.

So visibility rests on three things, and the report scores each:

  • Listings & profiles -- the same name, address, phone, hours and categories on Google, Bing, Apple and the directories your industry uses (e.g. HotDoc for dentists, hipages for tradies, OpenTable for restaurants).
  • Reviews & reputation -- recent, specific reviews on the platforms your customers read. Assistants repeat specifics ("fixed our burst pipe in an hour").
  • Website -- readable by AI search crawlers, and saying in plain text who you are, what you do, where, and when. Check yours free.
