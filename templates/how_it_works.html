How it works

1

Realistic questions, not your business name

We generate the kind of question a real customer would type -- "best {category} in {location}" -- and deliberately never mention your business by name, so the score reflects whether assistants recommend you unprompted.

2

Sent to five AI assistants in parallel

ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and DeepSeek each answer independently. Claude and Gemini are configured to use live web search where supported, closer to how a real user's question gets answered.

3

Answers are scored, not guessed

Each answer is checked for whether your business is named (matching is case-insensitive, whole-word, and tolerant of curly quotes and "&" vs "and"), and, if it's in a numbered or bulleted list, where it ranks. A mention with no clear ranking is counted but never force-fit into a fake position.

4

A plan tailored to your category and your gaps

The action plan combines category-specific playbooks (a dentist and a restaurant need different things) with notes on which specific assistant is under-performing for you and roughly why, based on how that assistant tends to source its answers.
