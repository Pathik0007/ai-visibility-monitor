Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ updated_date }}.

What we collect

A free, anonymous check submits the business name, category, location and any competitor names you type, purely to generate the report -- this is not tied to an identity and is only kept briefly (see below).

Creating an account stores your email address and a securely hashed password (never the password itself). If you subscribe, Stripe -- not us -- holds your payment details; we only store a subscription status and Stripe's own customer/subscription identifiers.

Saved businesses store the same business/category/location/competitor fields you provide, plus each check's results, so you can see your visibility history over time.

How long we keep it

An anonymous check's report is kept only long enough to make its link shareable and refreshable (currently 24 hours), then discarded. Saved-business history is kept for as long as your account exists.

Third parties

Running a check sends your submitted business details to whichever AI assistant APIs are configured (OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, Google, DeepSeek) purely to ask the test question -- the same way any tool that calls those APIs would. Payment processing, when enabled, is handled entirely by Stripe.

Your choices

You can use the free check without creating an account at all. If you have an account and want it and its data removed, contact us and we'll delete it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address listed on our sign-up page.
