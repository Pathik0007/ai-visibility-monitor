The service

AI Visibility Monitor tests whether AI assistants mention a given business in response to sample questions, and summarizes the result. Results reflect what those third-party assistants returned at the time of the check -- we don't control, and can't guarantee, what any assistant says at any given moment.

The action plan is general, good-faith guidance based on common patterns, not a guarantee that following it will change any assistant's output. Exactly how each assistant decides what to mention isn't published by its maker and can change at any time.
