About AI Visibility Monitor

More customers are asking ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and DeepSeek for a recommendation instead of scrolling a search engine or a maps app. That's a new kind of visibility that a business's usual SEO checklist doesn't directly measure.

AI Visibility Monitor asks each of those assistants the same realistic questions a real customer might ask -- "best dentist in Parramatta", "affordable plumber near me" -- and measures whether a given business gets named, how prominently, and which competitors come up instead.

From that, it builds a concrete action plan: general fixes that help across every assistant (a complete Google Business Profile, consistent listings, specific recent reviews), plus notes on how each individual assistant tends to source its answers, since a fix that helps with one doesn't always help with another.

The scoring is deliberately transparent about its own limits: an API failure is never counted as "not mentioned", and a prose-style answer isn't force-fit into a ranked position it doesn't have. See the FAQ for more detail on how it works.
