What we check

  • AI search crawlers allowed -- OAI-SearchBot (ChatGPT), Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Googlebot and Bingbot. Blocking these hides you from those answers.
  • Not hidden from search -- no accidental "noindex".
  • The basics in plain text -- business name, suburb, what you do, phone, opening hours. Many AI crawlers don't run JavaScript, so text has to be in the page.
  • Helpful extras -- business structured data, an FAQ, a clear title and description. Google says these aren't required, but they help assistants confirm your details.
