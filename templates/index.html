Why AI visibility matters now

More customers are asking ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews and Gemini for recommendations instead of scrolling search results. If an AI assistant doesn't know your business exists -- or recommends a competitor instead -- you lose that customer before they ever see your website. This tool runs realistic "best {category} near me" style questions against each assistant, tells you exactly how often you're mentioned versus your competitors, and what to fix if you're not.
